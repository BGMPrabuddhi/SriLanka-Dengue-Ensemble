## What the student (B. G. M. Prabuddhi) did
* Chose the problem (dengue early warning for Sri Lanka) and the data sources (denguedatahub, Open-Meteo).
* Wrote the project brief and requirements, and directed the work phase by phase.
* Made or approved the data and modelling decisions, e.g. weather lags of 1–8 weeks, keeping missing reports as
  missing (NaN + was-missing flags) rather than inventing values, choosing between raw and log1p targets on validation
  MAE, excluding the two most recent (possibly incomplete) weeks from evaluation, measuring every model against the
  naive baseline, and using recent-baseline thresholds in the dashboard.
* Reviewed the code, ran the notebooks and the dashboard, checked the outputs, and interpreted the results.
* Recorded the narrated video.
